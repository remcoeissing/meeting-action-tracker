# Projectbrief

Within our organization we have different committees of people that get together and have meetings. These committees are all focussed around a subject area and each meeting has an agenda. Attached to this agenda will be documents that participants are supposed to have read before joining. The end result will always be a summary and a list of next actions with assignees and due dates.

## Technology

We utilize the following technology stack:

* .NET 10
* NodeJS + Vite for Frontends
* API First Development
